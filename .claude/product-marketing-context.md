# Product Marketing Context

*Last updated: 2026-02-10*

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Multi-tenant platform for cycling teams to organize rides, manage routes, and communicate
**What it does:** Pedalons lets cycling clubs create teams, organize group rides with different skill levels, manage GPX routes with interactive maps, plan multi-day trips, and sync routes to GPS devices (Garmin, Hammerhead Karoo)
**Product category:** Cycling club management / Team organization software
**Product type:** SaaS (multi-tenant, white-label capable)
**Business model:** TBD

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** Cycling clubs, amateur cycling teams, recreational riding groups
**Decision-makers:** Club presidents, ride organizers, team administrators
**Primary use case:** Organizing and coordinating group cycling rides
**Jobs to be done:**
- Organize recurring group rides with multiple skill levels
- Share and manage cycling routes with team members
- Coordinate multi-day cycling trips
- Sync routes to GPS devices before rides

**Use cases:**
- Weekly club rides with beginner, intermediate, and advanced groups
- Multi-day cycling tours with stage-by-stage planning
- Route library management for a cycling club
- Member management and communication

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Cycling clubs struggle to organize rides across multiple tools (WhatsApp, email, Strava, spreadsheets)
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- WhatsApp/Facebook: No route management, no structured ride info, messages get lost
- Strava: Individual-focused, not designed for club coordination
- Spreadsheets: Manual, no GPS integration, poor mobile experience
**What it costs them:** Missed rides, confusion about meeting points, riders showing up to wrong group
**Emotional tension:** Frustration for organizers, anxiety for new members unsure which group to join
